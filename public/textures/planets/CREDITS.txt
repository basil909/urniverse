License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)

Sharper maps (public/textures/hd/, 4096x2048, loaded only for the planet in focus)
From Solar System Scope's 8k_mercury, 4k_venus_atmosphere, 8k_mars, 8k_jupiter,
8k_saturn, 8k_saturn_ring_alpha, 8k_earth_daymap, 8k_earth_clouds,
8k_earth_nightmap and 8k_moon - same source and licence as above (CC BY 4.0).
Downloaded 2026-11-10; resized to 4096 px wide and saved as WebP (the ring map
as PNG). Changes: Earth's oceans darkened to a deep blue; cloud and Moon tones
matched to the earlier maps; city lights extracted as for the 2K map above.

Earth and Moon 2K maps (public/textures/earth-day.webp, earth-clouds.webp, moon.webp)
Downscaled from the same 4K maps above, so the sharp swap only adds detail.

Relief (elevation) maps - public domain, downloaded 2026-11-10
- public/textures/relief/mars-height.webp: Mars MGS MOLA DEM global mosaic
  (USGS Astrogeology, mars_mgs_mola_dem_mosaic_global_1024.jpg), resampled to
  1024x512.
- public/textures/relief/mercury-height.webp: Mercury MESSENGER global DEM
  (USGS Astrogeology, mercury_messenger_dem_global_665m_1024.jpg), shifted
  180 degrees in longitude to match the colour map.
- public/textures/moon-height.webp: LRO LOLA elevation from NASA's Scientific
  Visualization Studio CGI Moon Kit (ldem_4_uint.tif), converted to 8-bit.
  Credit: NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio.
All saved as lossless WebP.

Galilean moon surfaces - public domain, USGS Astrogeology, downloaded 2026-11-10
- public/textures/moons/io.webp: Io Galileo SSI / Voyager colour merged global mosaic.
- public/textures/moons/europa.webp, ganymede.webp, callisto.webp: Voyager /
  Galileo SSI global mosaics (greyscale), no-data gaps filled and tinted to
  each moon's colour.

Phobos, Deimos and Titan surfaces - downloaded 2026-11-28, resampled to
1024 x 512, unlit / unmapped gaps filled from the surface around them, and
tinted to each moon's colour
- public/textures/moons/phobos.webp: Phobos Mars Express SRC Global Mosaic
  12m (Phobos_ME_SRC_Mosaic_Global_16ppd.tif), Mars Express SRC and Viking
  images. Public domain, USGS Astrogeology; ESA/DLR/FU Berlin, NASA.
- public/textures/moons/deimos.webp: Deimos map from Viking images
  (mar2kuu2.tif), NASA JPL map catalogue. Credit: Caltech/JPL/USGS.
- public/textures/moons/titan.webp: Titan Cassini ISS Global Mosaic 4005m
  (Titan_ISS_P19658_Mosaic_Global_4km.tif), Cassini near-infrared (938 nm)
  images, recentred on longitude 0 and drawn faintly (Titan's surface is
  hidden by its haze in visible light). Public domain, USGS Astrogeology;
  NASA/JPL-Caltech/Space Science Institute.
